TOPIC 01

History of the Internet

Learn how the Internet originated, developed and became an important part of modern communication and technology.

01 • OVERVIEW

What is the Internet?

The Internet is a worldwide network of interconnected computers and devices that communicate with each other using standard communication protocols.

It allows users to share information, communicate, access services and use applications from different locations around the world.

02 • HISTORY

Evolution of the Internet

1960s

ARPANET

ARPANET was developed as an early packet-switching network and became an important foundation for the modern Internet.

1970s

TCP/IP Development

The development of TCP/IP provided a standard way for different networks to communicate with each other.

1980s

Expansion of Networks

Networking technologies expanded and TCP/IP became increasingly important for interconnected networks.

1990s

World Wide Web

The World Wide Web made it easier for people to access and share information through web pages and browsers.

Today

Modern Internet

The Internet is now used for communication, education, business, entertainment, cloud computing and many other services.

03 • IMPORTANT POINTS

Key Points to Remember

  • The Internet is a global network of interconnected networks.
  • ARPANET played an important role in the early development of computer networking.
  • TCP/IP provides important communication protocols used on the Internet.
  • The World Wide Web made information accessible through web pages and browsers.
  • The Internet and the World Wide Web are related but are not the same thing.
